About Us

history of organisation:

Khalifa’s Design Store was founded in 2018 originally known as fearless wear, inspired by his live for fashion and unique sense of style developed during his high school years. While in college, he decided to turn this passion into a brand, gradually growing it by sharing his personal vision of fashion.

Mission statement:

Khalifa’s Design Store is committed to creating bold, unique fashion that empowers individuals to express themselves confidently and fearlessly. We aim to deliver high quality, original designs that inspire selfexpression and celebrate personal style in every customer
